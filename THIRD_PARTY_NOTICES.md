# Third-party components

`tutus/_vendor/lmms_eval` is the subset of lmms-eval 0.3.0 used for the five paper utility benchmarks, with this project's model adapters. Its MIT license is retained in `tutus/_vendor/LICENSE.lmms-eval`. The upstream project is https://github.com/EvolvingLMMs-Lab/lmms-eval.

Custom model implementations in `tutus/modeling/modelzoo` derive from Hugging Face Transformers and retain their original Apache-2.0 copyright and license headers. Model weights, dataset images and the YOLO face detector retain their source terms and are downloaded separately.
